What I care about

The principles behind my work.

01

Make security structural

A control that depends on someone remembering it will eventually be forgotten. I would rather spend an extra week making a mistake impossible in the types, the API, or the defaults than catch it in review forever.

02

Build for other engineers

A platform earns its trust on someone's worst day, not their first. If the safe path is slower than the shortcut, people take the shortcut, so the reliable option has to also be the easy one.

03

Use AI with discipline

I run agents against my own repositories, where a bad result costs me and nobody else. It is the only way I have found to tell durable leverage from a convincing demo.
